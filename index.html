Logistics

Getting to Campus

Much of campus closes to vehicle traffic during graduation weekend, so please allow extra travel time.

🅿️

Parking

Parking is free for all attendees at two locations: the PS7 structure at the Knight Management Center, and Lots 9 & 10 near the Galvez Lot (follow signs for "Frost Parking"). If you plan to attend the receptions at Knight Management Center, PS7 keeps you closest.

Disability parking: Roth Way, between Palm Drive and Lasuen Street.

🚗

Rideshare & Drop-Off

The rideshare and drop-off / pick-up area for the GSB ceremony is at the corner of Palm Drive and Roth Way. The main entrance to Frost Amphitheater is the gate at Lasuen Way & Roth Way.

From SFO ≈ 35 min · From SJC ≈ 25 min · From Palo Alto ≈ 10 min

Before You Come

Good to Know

The essentials that make for a smooth, stress-free day on campus.

🎟️ Tickets

The Saturday GSB ceremony is open to all guests — no ticket needed. Sunday's University Commencement requires a ticket per guest, and I've already secured yours for the ticketed portions of the weekend. Just let me know you're coming and I'll make sure you're set.

👜 Clear-Bag Policy

A clear-bag policy and metal-detector screening are in effect at both Frost Amphitheater and Stanford Stadium. Carry only essentials — there's no bag check for prohibited items. Be ready to remove phones, keys, and large metal objects at the gate.

👗 What to Wear

Attire is not formal — think "church or garden party." Dress comfortably for warm weather and full sun, with shoes good for some walking. No ties necessary!

💐 Flowers & Leis

Leis and bouquets can be pre-ordered (recommended) or bought on-site. The booth is at the Lasuen Street entrance of Frost on Saturday (3–7 PM) and at Gate 5 of Stanford Stadium on Sunday (7:30 AM–12 PM).

🥂 Food & Drink

Water is free inside all venues. Light snacks and sodas are sold at Frost's concession area. Champagne and sweets follow Saturday's ceremony; a buffet luncheon follows Sunday's.

📸 Photography

A professional photographer will capture me receiving my diploma on stage. You're welcome to take photos and video from your seats — just stay behind the barriers and out of the graduate areas.

♿ Accessibility

Disability and accessibility accommodations are available for both ceremonies — just let me know in advance what you need and I'll arrange it. Disability parking is on Roth Way.

📺 Can't Make It?

Celebrate with us from anywhere — watch the livestream here. The GSB webcast starts Saturday at 4:55 PM PDT; the University ceremony also streams on the commencement site and KZSU 90.1 FM. Follow along with #GSBgrads.

Let Me Know You're Coming

I've already secured tickets for the ticketed portions of the weekend — so all that's left is for you to tell me which events you'll join. Can't be here in person? Tune in to the livestream and celebrate with us from wherever you are.
